# V9 review

Verdict: Borderline. Sixteen generated high-score sources are square and the browser creation preview loads the expected female and male score-8 files. Hair remains largely broad ink masses. Faces have more modeled eyes and smoother shading than the coarser v8 middle tiers; several characters retain a similar facial structure or hairstyle to their v8 counterpart. Their appeal and whether the finer rendering fits the desired style remain subjective and should be judged against the contact sheet. Some hair silhouettes run close to the image edge. No artwork was manually redrawn.

The contact sheet is arranged female 08, female 10, male 08, male 10, with four variants per row. The score-8 creation preview was checked for each gender; asset selection tests cover all 48 combinations, but the score-10 portraits were not individually inspected in the running UI.
