# V9 high-appearance portraits

Each high-tier character is generated individually from the V8 brush-and-paper swatch, with a separately designed face, pose and hairstyle. The generated face is more expressive while hair stays in coarse ink masses. Lower-tier V8 originals remain unchanged.

## Shared prompt

Create ONE original full-square Chinese wuxia Jianghu character avatar. The image is ONLY a swatch for broad black ink brush and pale neutral grey-ivory rice paper; do not copy any object or face from it.
The user's highest-appearance characters must feel striking and memorable, not plain or generic. Make this adult character unmistakably beautiful/handsome through elegantly balanced facial proportions, distinctive expressive eyes with a tiny natural glint, beautifully shaped brows, a refined nose and mouth, and a vivid individual expression. Beauty must be apparent in the face, not attributed merely to rich costume. Make the person and personality clearly different from other characters and from the opposite gender.
Visual medium: coarse hand-painted old Chinese wuxia ink-and-muted-watercolor manhua, expressive slightly uneven dark brush contours and flat restrained washes on light neutral grey-ivory paper. Hair MUST consist of 3–5 large SOLID dark ink shapes, NO individually drawn hair strands, no wispy flyaways, fine hatching or tiny highlights. A FEW expressive facial brush marks and a soft restrained color wash are allowed to communicate beauty, but no photorealistic skin, pores, detailed eyelashes, glossy airbrushed gradients or 3D.
Square head-and-collar avatar: entire hair silhouette, ornaments, face and chin within image, with 6% visible pale-paper margin around every edge; head and hair occupy roughly 84–90% of image height, narrow collar and tiny shoulder hints only at bottom. No chest, torso, arms, hands, scenery, yellow paper, vignette, border, lettering or watermark.

Character-specific prompts are in `characters.json`. Female 10 variant 02 was a warm, lively female heroine with a broad side coil, wine-red ribbon and pale teal/red collar. Male 10 variant 02 was an elegant male scholar with a broad half-up coil, a jade pin and ivory/slate-blue collar. Both used the same paper-and-brush swatch and avoided individual hair strands.
