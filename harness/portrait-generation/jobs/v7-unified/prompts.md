# V7 portraits: shared paper and prompts

The image input is the blank crop `paper-reference.png`, used only for paper color and texture so it does not impose any face, hairstyle or gender on the characters. The built-in image-generation tool produced each full-size portrait.

## Shared prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Create ONE original square Chinese wuxia MUD character portrait. The input is a blank paper sample ONLY: match its exact light neutral grey-ivory rice-paper background and delicate fine fibers in the entire background. No yellow, ochre, brown tint, gradient, dark vignette, scene, border or text.
Art medium: expressive hand-painted old Chinese Jianghu ink-and-watercolor portrait, dark uneven ink-brush contours with visible dry-brush breaks, broad ink hair masses, lightly washed faces and restrained matte mineral watercolors, moderate detail. Beautiful facial features should look striking while staying illustrated, not a photograph or slick digital anime. Each character should have a different face, hairstyle, expression, pose and distinctive colored collar. Beauty comes from facial structure and expression, not clothing condition or wealth.
STRICT COMPOSITION: square head-and-collar portrait only. Face plus hair must visually occupy about 84-90% of the image height. Clothing is only a narrow robe-collar strip plus tiny shoulder hints in the bottom 6-12%; for an explicit over-shoulder/back-turn view it may reach 18% at most. No chest, torso mass, waist, broad shoulders, arms, hands, forearms, elbows, full sleeves, or action-pose limbs. Do not turn it into a bust, half-body, waist-up portrait, or cropped UI icon.
Keep the complete hair silhouette and chin inside the square with margin, never cut them off. No UI frame, lettering, logo, watermark, ornate fantasy armor, modern objects or photorealism.
